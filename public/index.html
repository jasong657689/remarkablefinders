Our Story

Born from a Love of
Cardboard.

It started in 1986 — saving up allowance money for packs of baseball cards, hoping to pull a Don Mattingly or Kirby Puckett. Walking into card shops with long rows of cases, flipping through binders looking for that last card to complete a set.

"The joy you felt when you bought that last card to complete a set was so exciting."

That feeling never left. So in 2016, we started selling on eBay with a mission most sellers skip: list the commons, price them fairly, keep shipping reasonable — and help real collectors build real sets. No dollar minimums. Just a collector helping collectors.
